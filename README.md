### `> about`

I spend most of my time understanding how systems work — not just that they work. My learning comes from picking up problems that seem too big, shipping something half-broken, and working backward.

I'm drawn to spaces where disciplines collide: ML systems that interact with financial markets, blockchain infrastructure for agricultural supply chains, applied AI where deployment matters as much as accuracy.

Currently doing applied ML research at **[MindstriX](https://www.mindstrix.in/)** — working on production ML pipelines end-to-end.
Studying **B.Tech Electronics & Telecommunication** at **PICT Pune** (2024–2028).
