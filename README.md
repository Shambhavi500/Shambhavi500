### `> whoami`

I like understanding how things work — not just that they work.

Most of what I've learned came from picking up a problem that seemed too big, building something half-broken, and working backward from what went wrong. I'm drawn to systems where the interesting complexity lives at the intersection of domains: markets, agriculture, machine learning, distributed infrastructure.

I'm studying Electronics and Telecommunication at PICT Pune, and currently doing applied ML research at MindstriX. But more than any single label, I'm someone who learns by shipping.

`AI / ML` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Blockchain` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Financial systems` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Agritech` &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Distributed systems`
